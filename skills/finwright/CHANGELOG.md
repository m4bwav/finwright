# Changelog: finwright

Every change to [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and its companions, newest first, each with the reason. Reasons cite findings in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) (`R-`), lessons in [LEARNINGS.md](LEARNINGS.md) (`L-`), and test runs in [TESTS.md](TESTS.md) (`T-`). State in `evergreen.json`. Protocol: MAINTENANCE.md.

Entry shape: `### C-YYYYMMDD-n · date · one-line summary`, then `because:` (IDs or "user request"), `files:` (file and section), and a sentence on what changed. Cite section headings, not line numbers.

### C-20261021-1 · 2026-10-21 · Inbox sweep: fw.py mail scan, sender and report; inbox-sweep reference
- because: user request (read several mailboxes and track every retirement account mention, which account is where and for which job; "others might want someone to sort their financial situation out of their inbox"), web checks 2026-10-21 (Gmail's POP fetch and Gmailify retired in 2026; Thunderbird 148 signs in to Yahoo, AOL and AT&T with OAuth2; Yahoo has no built-in mbox export)
- files: scripts/fw_mail.py (new), scripts/fw.py (mail subcommands, version), SKILL.md (description, Other jobs, References), references/inbox-sweep.md (new), evals/evals.json (trigger-7), tests, AGENTS.md, README.md, plugin.json, .claude-plugin/plugin.json
- `fw.py mail scan` reads mbox files (Takeout, Thunderbird folder files with no extension), .eml files, folders of them, and JSON Lines saved from a mail connector, filters raw bytes cheaply before parsing, and keeps one redacted row per matching message in `<data>/mail/mentions.csv` (sender, date, terms, plan names, last-4 hints, amounts mentioned, a short context; no bodies). Job ads that list a 401(k) as a benefit are dropped. Institutions come from the user's own accounts and hunt rows and a private `mail-senders.csv` (`fw.py mail sender`); no institution list ships, because a list of well-known firms would reveal which ones a maintainer uses. `fw.py mail report` groups by institution with dates, kinds, plans, account endings and links to hunt rows and accounts, and lists unknown senders as `? domain`. The reference gives a route per provider and the connector searches; the skill never takes an email password, since an email account resets every financial login. Version 0.9.0.

### C-20261017-1 · 2026-10-17 · Recommend with reasons; new listings (ipo and position commands, new-listings reference)
- because: user request (run an IPO buying plan through finwright, improve it and release), user request ("part of finwright's mission is giving solid and reasonable financial advice"), R-20261017-1, T-20261017-1
- files: scripts/fw.py (ipo, position, big, positive, short, quarter_end; latest_holdings takes --as-of; DISCLAIMER), SKILL.md (description, hard rule 6, Other jobs, References), references/new-listings.md (new), references/next-dollar.md (Investing, How to answer), references/rules.md (rule 15), research/2026-10-17-ipo-buying.md (new), research/README.md, RESEARCH.md, evals/evals.json (trigger-6, outcome-1), README.md, plugin.json, .claude-plugin/plugin.json and marketplace.json (descriptions), tests
- The skill now recommends: after the numbers it says what it would do in the user's place, with the evidence, the user's figures, the main risk and what would change the answer, and names any stake the model or its maker has; the user still decides and nothing is executed. The disclaimer now reads "Guidance from your own records and published research, not a licensed adviser's advice; you decide." `fw.py ipo` turns company values or a prospectus price range and share count into per-share prices, market values, revenue multiples (flagging over 40 times, where Ritter's IPOs did worst) and the dates after listing (analysts at 10 days, the first quarter end, the lock-up). `fw.py position` shows a single-stock buy against investable, liquid and total net worth with 5% and 10% concentration bands, the emergency fund after it, and a ladder of limit orders below a reference price (whole or fractional shares, rounded down so the total never exceeds the amount). A fresh-eyes review before release found 16 issues (a ladder starting with a negative step failed on Python 3.10 and 3.11, comma lists, unchecked negative and zero inputs, output before validation, a cent of overspend, `--as-of` ignored for holdings); all were fixed and tested, and the lists became space-separated (`--valuation 1.5T 2T`, `--below 0 10 20`). Version 0.8.0; fw.py about nineteen hundred lines, still yellow and close to the red line: the next feature should come with the importer split proposed in the handoff.

### C-20261015-1 · 2026-10-15 · Where the next dollar goes: mortgage and compare commands, next-dollar reference
- because: user request (what to do with the next $10,000: I bonds, investing, paying off a nearly paid mortgage or cash), R-20261015-1
- files: scripts/fw.py (mortgage, compare, level_payment, amortize, month_after, compare_option), SKILL.md (description, Other jobs, References), references/next-dollar.md (new), research/2026-10-15-next-dollar-literature.md (new), research/README.md, RESEARCH.md, evals/evals.json (trigger-5), tests
- `fw.py mortgage` prints the payoff month, the interest left and what a lump sum or monthly extra principal saves on a fixed-rate loan, from the mortgage account's latest balance or `--balance`, with the payment given or worked out from `--original` and `--term`; it says that a partial prepayment frees no monthly cash until payoff and that escrow goes on. `fw.py compare` ranks the after-tax yields of fixed-rate choices (`NAME=RATE:none|full|federal|deductible`, or `ibond` for the current composite from rates.json) and the value of the sum after N years. references/next-dollar.md holds the questions to ask first and what the forums, blogs and research say. Version 0.7.0; fw.py about 1,730 lines, still yellow.

### C-20261014-1 · 2026-10-14 · QFX/OFX import, time-weighted return, fee drag, percent-only view; holdings fixes
- because: research/2026-10-12-data-access-routes.md (QFX downloads are the route every institution still offers), research/2026-10-12-net-worth-trackers.md (Empower's fee analyzer, Ghostfolio's restricted view, idempotent imports), the roadmap, T-20261014-1
- files: scripts/fw.py (import ofx, ofx_tree, ofx_statements; twr in returns; fees --project, --growth, --admin-fee; networth --percent-only; add_balances, replace_holdings, keep_expense_ratios, parse_classes, signed_balance; history), SKILL.md (description, the net worth check, Other jobs), references/data-routes.md (QFX and OFX downloads), references/valuation.md (Returns), tests
- Added `fw.py import ofx` for QFX and OFX downloads in both dialects: bank and card balances, and brokerage positions with cash, matched to accounts by last 4, duplicates skipped. `returns` now prints a chain-linked Modified Dietz time-weighted return beside XIRR, not annualised under a year. `fees --project` shows what fund expenses and the plan's admin fee take over 10, 20 and 30 years at an assumed growth rate. `networth --percent-only` shows shares of assets with no amounts or account names. Version 0.6.0; fw.py is about 1,600 lines, in the yellow size band.
