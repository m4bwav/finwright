# Research

The first research pass behind finwright, 2026-10-12. Every claim in these reports has a source URL and a check date; anything time-sensitive may have changed since. The skill's own [RESEARCH.md](../skills/finwright/RESEARCH.md) keeps the claims it depends on and the re-check plan.

- [AI tools and MCP landscape](2026-10-12-ai-tools-and-mcp-landscape.md): read when choosing an MCP server, app or AI feature, or checking whether something already does a job.
- [Net worth trackers](2026-10-12-net-worth-trackers.md): read when adding a report or feature; it lists what commercial and open-source trackers do well and what users complain about.
- [Data access routes](2026-10-12-data-access-routes.md): read before adding an import or connector for any institution. Some banks have since ended QFX/OFX downloads (Bank of America on 2025-09-30; see the Bank of America section of [data-routes.md](../skills/finwright/references/data-routes.md)).
- [Method, rules and security](2026-10-12-method-rules-and-security.md): read before changing how anything is valued, or anything touching credentials, privacy or advice.
- [Consequential-action safeguards](2026-10-12-consequential-action-safeguards.md): read before connecting any MCP server, browser automation or vendor account that can write, and before changing finwright's hooks or permission advice.
- [Finding old 401(k)s](2026-10-12-finding-old-401ks.md): read when tracing retirement accounts from former employers (Lost and Found, SSA-L99, Form 5500, safe-harbor IRAs, unclaimed property) or adding a per-employer search record.
- [Monarch skill learnings](2026-10-12-monarch-skill-learnings.md): read before connecting Monarch (official MCP or CSV download) or importing its balances or holdings; auth risks, GraphQL fields, type map, CSV headers, pitfalls.
- [Monarch web survey](2026-10-12-monarch-web-survey.md): read before connecting Monarch or writing the guard rules for it; the official MCP is paused since 2026-06-29, its 41 tool names, CSV export columns, holdings have no export, and sources beyond GitHub.
- [Where the next dollar goes](2026-10-15-next-dollar-literature.md) (2026-10-15): read before answering "pay off the mortgage, I bonds, cash or invest"; the forum and blog consensus, the research, I bond timing, the safe yields of mid-October 2026 and where the sources disagree.
- [Buying a new listing (IPO)](2026-10-17-ipo-buying.md) (2026-10-17): read before answering "should I buy the IPO, when, at what price, how much"; Ritter's first-day and three-year returns, lock-ups, retail access and flipping rules, first-day order rules, single-stock concentration, staged buying and pre-IPO markets.
